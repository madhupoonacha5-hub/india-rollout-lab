Inspect counts and metric definitions

Activation. Unique learners with a first lesson completion in the first 24 hours, divided by learners with a complete 24-hour observation window.

Day-7 retention. Unique learners with an activity event from 168 to under 192 hours after onboarding, divided by learners whose full day-7 window elapsed.

Cohorts are synthetic and non-randomized. The comparison is descriptive. It cannot establish a causal lift or production readiness.
