Model assumptions and decision thresholds

All inputs and outcomes are synthetic. The model uses 10,000 eligible free learners. The control has 400 trials, 100 paid starts at ₹299, and 30% day-7 engagement measured across all assigned learners, regardless of subscription. Incremental gross monthly revenue compares modeled paid starts × test price against ₹29,900 for control. This excludes fees, refunds, AI costs, and renewals. Early offers increase exposure and reduce engagement in this scenario. Local examples and later offers change the modeled trial and paid funnel. The draft gate requires at least 30% day-7 engagement and ₹5,000 incremental gross monthly revenue before recommending a limited pilot.
